Shinobi no Sho Revamp — Kuchiyose patch 1.6.4

Changes:
- Corrected chakra deduction to system.energias.chakra.value (the actual Revamp data model).
- Summons are positioned beside the summoner's token with footprint collision checks.
- Size selector offers six core creature sizes: Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge, Immense, subject to NC/species restrictions.
- Summoned actor stores NC and size/reach, and its prototype and spawned token use the selected size.
- Added Doki (Club), Doki (Claws), Doki (Armless) as separate dropdown choices and supplied token artwork.
- Doki (Claws) uses Tekko-kagi: Advanced Shinobi Guide pp. 69–72, +3/+1, critical 15–16, Slashing/Piercing.
- Doki (Club) uses Club; Doki (Armless) uses Unarmed Strike.
- Existing Doki variants share the Doki blood contract.

Limitations:
- Size modifiers are stored on the summon profile, not fully integrated with all roll formulas.
- The source profile for Doki does not provide complete stats or club weapon numerical data.
- NC 4+ attribute point distribution, half-Vitality creature formula and combat/Command Partner automation still need system-level implementation.
- GM required for spawning and chakra deduction; player control is inherited where actor ownership is configured.
- Already-created summon actors/tokens are not retroactively updated.
- Not tested in a live Foundry world.
