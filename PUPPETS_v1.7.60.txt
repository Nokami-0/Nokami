Puppets — Initial Types — v1.7.60

Adds a separate PUPPETS character-sheet tab and a Puppets shortcut in the
Kuchiyose panel. The GM’s startup builds a separate Actor compendium:
Puppets — Initial Puppet Types. System Settings provides a manual build menu.
Existing generated templates are not duplicated or overwritten on repeat builds.

Source: Book of Hijutsu Vol. 2, printed pp. 101–102 (PDF pp. 102–103), with
construction, control and device rules from printed pp. 86–96.

Three Medium examples, each with 1 Light, 2 Standard and 1 Heavy slot:
Offensive: Light Flamethrower; Standard Water Jet and Flamethrower;
Heavy Advanced Machinery. Specialist (Devices), Blind Spot references.
Balanced: Light Smoke Bombs; Standard Hidden Knives and Water Jet;
Heavy Body Division. Specialist (Devices), Fighter references.
Support: Light Smoke Bombs; Standard Chakra Shield and Elastic Limb;
Heavy Chest (Mechanics 10 prerequisite). Fighter, Blind Spot, Diehard references.

On the Puppets tab, Create Actor makes an owned copy associated with that
character as controller. Owned puppets can be opened or dragged onto the canvas
directly from that tab. It creates no animal contract, spends no chakra or Ryo,
and grants no aptitudes. Actual aptitude purchases still use the controller’s
power points. Actor creation requires Foundry’s actor-create permission.

Each custom puppet sheet has Overview, Devices and Puppet Rules tabs.
It reads controller Mechanics, Spirit, Ranged Combat, Dodge and Anticipate.
Body Hardness: 1. Medium Absorption: 5 × Mechanics, not creature Vitality.
Damage reference: ceil(Mechanics / 2 + Weapon Damage). Limited device charges
are tracked independently; repair and village-resupply controls require user
confirmation that their source requirements were completed.

First controller assignment initializes Absorption. Subsequent edits do not
provide an automatic repair. Drag an actor to the canvas for its selectable,
unlinked token. Token character-HP bars are hidden to avoid confusing them with
puppet Absorption. Use the custom sheet for Absorption and device charges.

Thread connection costs, Kurohigi quantity/action limits, device-specific chakra,
actions, effects, aptitude modifiers and damage application remain manual.
The sheet’s attack/defense rolls use the controller’s current base precision.
The two legacy Initial Puppet reference cards now contain the actual three
loadouts; rebuilding organized item compendiums updates those old cards.

Validation: 55 automated checks passed, including exact loadouts, device slots,
controller-based values, charge depletion, compendium idempotence/locking,
creation ownership, custom-sheet rendering, controller assignment and existing
actor/summon/Magen/Senjutsu/Tensai regressions. Live Foundry testing remains.
