Kuchiyose: Rashoumon — v1.7.54

As GM, rebuild the Kuchiyose compendium after updating and restarting.
On a summoner's sheet: Kuchiyose > Summon Creature > Rashoumon (Rashomon).
Click Summon to open the gate controls. Own the purchased Rashoumon aptitude;
attributes alone do not grant its higher levels.

Purchased level 1: Spirit or Intelligence 8; maximum 1 active gate.
Purchased level 2: Spirit or Intelligence 12; maximum 3 active gates; +2 Hardness.
Purchased level 3: Spirit or Intelligence 18; maximum 5 active gates; +3 Hardness
instead of the level-2 bonus. Insufficient damage does not degrade these gates.

Choose Spirit or Intelligence for all parameters. Width and height are each
half that attribute in metres; range is 5 m plus that attribute. Casting costs
half the attribute, rounded up to a whole chakra point. Divide total Hardness
across gates cast together. One gate at levels 2–3 uses a Partial Action;
otherwise use a Movement Action.

Active-gate limits include existing gate tokens on all scenes. Remove tokens
when dismissed, destroyed or the scene ends to free those slots. Wall placement
uses the scene scale: width is measured on the map, physical height appears on
the gate sheet. One-square token depth is a visual marker, not a rule for wall
thickness. You may reposition/rotate the markers into a legal arrangement.

The gate sheet tracks remaining Hardness independently for each unlinked token.
Defense (Magic Level +2), damage passing through destroyed gates, confinement
checks, opening/closing once per round, scene-end dismissal, and obstruction by
walls require manual resolution. Gates do not automatically create Foundry Walls
or block attacks/movement. No creature NC, blood contract or ordinary creature
summoning scene usage is consumed by this separate common aptitude.

Source: bundled Hijutsu Volume 2 entries, PDF pages 141–143.
Automated checks cover prerequisites, gate caps, dimensions, cost, placement,
rollback, gate-sheet rendering, and existing creature summoning regressions.
