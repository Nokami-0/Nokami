Special Weapons from Hijutsus 4.1.b, Raijin no Ken Revised and Seimei Set Revised. 14 entries (9+1+4) are created as world Items under Shinobi no Sho — Weapons / Special Weapons when a GM loads the world. Existing same-named entries in that folder are preserved. Drag items from the Items sidebar onto a character. Fixed weapon damage, critical 15–16, slot sizes and book-specific rules are included. Kusanagi has variable damage (0 placeholder). Hiramekarei and paired swords list per-blade damage. Mugen Kōgai is armor represented as a special equipment item and does not automatically modify defenses. Active powers/charges and complex conditions are descriptive and require manual adjudication; do not claim fully automated. Foundry runtime not available for live testing.
