SENJUTSU (SAGE TECHNIQUE) — v1.7.57

Adds a dedicated Senjutsu tab to ordinary character sheets, alongside Magen.
Update and restart the world; no compendium rebuild is required.

33 source-backed reference cards cover purchase prerequisites, the Kuchiyose
and Mokuton routes, Sage Mode entry/duration, separate Senjutsu Chakra,
Sensor, all nine spendable bonuses, four combat recharge methods, reverse
summoning, chakra-draining petrification, Two Lives and its symbiosis rules,
perfected appearance, and ten per-level chakra-capacity milestones.

Filters select exact level, category or text. The tab shows the owned Senjutsu
power level and its corresponding chakra capacity when that power is detected.
Vigor eligibility is shown separately from the required qualifying route.
No prerequisite, power, aptitude, clone or bonus is granted by viewing a card.

Source: Basic Book 4.1.b, printed pages 239–241. The conflicting source entry
header (3 turns) and body text (end of second concentration round) are noted
for GM confirmation. Two Lives has its own explicit 3-turn entry process.
Optional summon routes require GM permission; Slugs do not grant access.

This is a readable reference tab, matching the Magen implementation. Activation,
Senjutsu Chakra spending, once-per-scene benefits, turns, concentration,
clones, recharge and condition application remain manual. Magen and creature
summoning sheets are preserved. A missing rules asset is reported in the tab
without preventing the rest of the actor sheet from opening.

Validation: 33 checks passed for source coverage, levels, prerequisites, filters,
rendering, failed-load recovery and actor/summon/Magen regressions.
Live Foundry testing remains.
