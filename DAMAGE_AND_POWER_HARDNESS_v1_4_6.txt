v1.4.6: Take Damage button applies total Hardness, armor absorption pool, character absorption, then Vitality. Bypass toggles for special cases. Power/Jutsu items now have a Hardness bonus and an Active checkbox. Only explicitly active powers contribute to actor total Hardness. Durations and automatic activation are not implemented. Character Absorption is treated as per-hit damage reduction, not a consumable pool. Back up your world before installing.
