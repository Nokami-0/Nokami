MAGEN (DEMONIC ILLUSION) — v1.7.56

A dedicated MAGEN tab appears on ordinary character sheets after updating and
restarting the world. No compendium rebuild is required to load this reference.

Includes 16 base effects, 22 evolutions spanning levels 1–10, six core-rules
cards and the separately purchased Fluid Illusion limited aptitude. Filter by
level, illusion type or text. Click a card heading to expand its full rules.
Magen remains a restricted Hijutsu, separate from common Ninpou.

The tab displays an owned Magen power's level, Intelligence, normal resistance
DC, touch resistance DC and range when it can identify that purchased power.
The effect cards show effect-level chakra costs and action exceptions such as
False Speed's 2 Chakra cost. Deep Illusion is already included in the DC.
Viewing a card does not buy an effect or grant its prerequisites.
Manage purchased powers on the existing Powers tab.

Reference source: Basic Book 4.1.b, printed pages 233–238. English effect names
are cleaned and two ambiguous translated condition descriptions are explicitly
marked for GM confirmation. Levels 6–10 contain evolutions rather than invented
new base effects. Resistance, detection, Kai, durations and conditions remain
manual; this update adds reference presentation rather than combat automation.

If the rules asset cannot load, its error appears in the Magen panel and the
rest of the actor sheet remains available. Reopening retries the load.
Filters do not submit or modify actor data. Summon sheet tabs are preserved.

Validation: 25 automated checks passed: catalog coverage, restrictions, exact
level/type filtering, cost exceptions, DCs, escaped rendering, failure recovery,
filter controls and actor/summon opening regressions. Live Foundry testing remains.
