Based on Shinobi no Sho 4.1.b pp.127-129,257. Adds weapon data fields, quantity per compartment, equip toggle and damage calculator: ceil(attribute/2)+weapon damage+bonus, multiplied by chosen damage degree 1-4. Encumbrance penalties shown only, not automatically applied. Attack rolls, ammunition, proficiencies, prerequisites, defenses and criticals are not automated. Revamp-specific differences unverified.
