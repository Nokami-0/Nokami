TENSAI (GENIUS) — v1.7.58

Adds the Tensai tab to ordinary character sheets alongside Magen and Senjutsu.
Update and restart the world; no compendium rebuild is required.

38 source-backed reference cards cover Versatility and Hibon Ninpou routes,
Versatility choices at levels 1–10, three limited aptitudes, eight special-aptitude
base/upgrade entries, three clan references, and eight Hiraishin technique sections.

Versatility progression preserves both level-1 effects, the allowed seven-power
list, two different chosen powers, and no more than two consecutive effect-choice
levels assigned to the same power. Hibon Ninpou is an alternative to Versatility.
Special Aptitude level-2 upgrades are distinguished from Versatility levels and
do not use more of the maximum two Special Aptitude slots.

Clan references remain Hatake (Silver Fang, p.155), Sarutobi (Will of Fire, p.174),
and Senju (Maximize, p.175). Their underlying clan compendium entries are not moved
or recategorized. Individual eligibility and prerequisites still apply.

Source: Basic Book 4.1.b, pp.241–247, with Hibon Ninpou p.212 and the cited clan
pages. Teleportation transport sizes use the corrected Medium/Large/Immense names.

The tab shows owned Versatility/Hibon power entries, category and level filters,
search and expandable rules. This is a reference: purchases, effect choices,
special slots, seals, costs and Hiraishin movement remain manual. Reading a card
does not grant abilities. Failed rule loads show an error without blocking the
rest of the sheet; reopening retries the load.

Validation: 41 checks passed for catalog coverage, progression, clan references,
rendering, filters, source costs and Magen/Senjutsu/actor/summon regressions.
Live Foundry testing remains.
