Equipment fields are now in the Foundry Item TypeDataModel schema so they persist. Damage calculation: Basic Book pp.257-258, ceil(attribute/2)+weapon damage+modifier (minimum zero), multiplied by 2d8 attack result degree: 2-3 fail, 4-8 degree 1, 9-11 degree 2, 12-14 degree 3, 15-16 degree 4 (with configurable critical threshold). Other attack/defense rules are not automated. Backup world first. Not tested live in Foundry.
