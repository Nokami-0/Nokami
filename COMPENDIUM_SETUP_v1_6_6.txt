Shinobi no Sho Revamp v1.6.6 — Native Drag-and-Drop Compendiums

1. Install this system in Data/systems/shinobi-no-sho-revamp.
2. Restart Foundry and open a world as GM.
3. Game Settings > Configure Settings > System Settings >
   Shinobi Compendiums — Clans, Powers, Techniques & Aptitudes > Build Drag-and-Drop Compendiums.
4. Click Build Compendiums and wait for completion.
5. Open the Compendium Packs sidebar; six world Item packs appear.
6. Drag entries onto an open Shinobi no Sho character sheet.

The original descriptions, system data and source flags are preserved. The categories
are inferred from existing catalog metadata; some entries may require manual review.
No complete mechanical automation of prerequisites, chakra or effects is implied.

Because these are created as world compendiums at runtime, they are not prebuilt LevelDB
packs embedded in the ZIP. Rebuilding skips existing records based on source IDs.

Existing summoning, images and world importer are retained. Untested in live Foundry.
